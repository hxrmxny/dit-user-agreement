Privacy Policy

In short

  • The bot reads messages only in project channels, to recognise text shortcuts such as создать главу 12. Ordinary chat is ignored and not stored.
  • It stores what a team needs to run its work: chapters, roles, schedules, balances and payout records, and the profile details you enter yourself.
  • Nothing is sold, used for advertising or used to train AI / machine-learning models.
  • You can stop the bot from reading your messages with /privacy, and ask for your data to be deleted at any time.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Discord bot DIT Stein (Discord application ID 1473814672225599663), shown on servers under a nickname such as «DIT | Заливка», together with its web panel (the “bot”). The bot is a workflow tool for manga translation teams: it keeps track of projects, chapters, role assignments, release schedules, pings and the team’s internal balances.

The bot is run by an independent developer (the “bot administrator”, “we”). It is not made or endorsed by Discord.

2. What data the bot processes

2.1 Discord account and server data

  • Your Discord user ID, username, display name and server nickname.
  • IDs of the server, the project channels and the bot’s own messages (for example chapter cards).
  • Server roles and which members hold them (Server Members intent). This builds departments from roles such as “English translator, S-rank”, keeps your rank in your nickname and draws the team roster image.

2.2 Information you enter yourself

  • Payout contacts, meaning where your team should send your money. Only your server’s treasurer and administrators can see them.
  • The free-text “About me” field.
  • Your settings: nickname colour on the roster image, vacation status, ping preferences and “Do not disturb”, and whether your rank shows in your nickname.

2.3 Work records

  • The titles you work on, chapters, the roles you are assigned, their status and release schedules.
  • Rates, balances, accruals, bonuses, payouts and the transaction log for your server.
  • Statistics built from these records, such as top contributors by role and uploads per platform.

2.4 Message content (Message Content intent)

In channels that are set up as projects, the bot reads new messages to recognise text shortcuts, for example где 12, создать главу 12-15, назначить @user Клин 5-12 or оплатить 614-650, together with any files or links attached to them, such as an archive of raw pages.

  • Messages that are not shortcuts are ignored. They are not saved, logged or analysed.
  • From a shortcut the bot saves only its result: chapter numbers, role assignments, links to files and the text of a note attached to a chapter.
  • The bot does not read messages in channels that are not projects.

2.5 Scripts (“blueprints”)

If you submit a script with /blueprints submit, the file and your user ID are kept so the developer can review it and so the team can run approved versions.

2.6 Technical logs

Error and event logs needed to keep the bot running. They are kept for up to 30 days.

2.7 What the bot does not collect

Your online status or activities, voice data, direct messages to other people, or messages outside project channels.

3. How the data is used

Only to provide the bot’s features to your team: creating and updating chapter cards, assigning roles and pinging the right people, schedules and the release tracker, departments and roster images, balances and payout records, statistics, and support when something breaks.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, build profiles for anyone outside your team, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your team on the same server sees chapter cards, role assignments and roster images. These are posted in Discord channels the team already has access to.
  • The treasurer and administrators of your server see balances, the transaction log and payout contacts.
  • Department leads see the members of their own department.
  • The bot administrator can access the database to run the bot and to answer support and deletion requests.

Data from one server is not shown to other servers. The web panel follows the same rules as the /team menu.

5. Third-party services

  • Discord, the platform the bot runs on. Discord’s own Privacy Policy applies to your use of Discord.
  • Hosting provider: the bot, its database and the web panel run on a rented virtual server (VPS).
  • Google Drive: if a team enables blueprints, raw chapter files are saved to that team’s Google Drive. No personal data about members is sent there.
  • MangaLib and other source sites: the bot checks public pages to see whether a chapter is out. No personal data is sent to them.

6. Storage and security

Data is stored in a database on the bot’s server, on an encrypted disk partition, so it is encrypted at rest. Only the bot administrator can access the server and the database. Payout contacts are shown only to the treasurer and administrators of your server. We only collect what the features above need.

7. How long data is kept

  • Work records and profile data are kept while your server uses the bot.
  • Technical logs are kept for up to 30 days.
  • If you ask us to delete your data, we do it within 30 days (see below).
  • If a server stops using the bot, its administrator can ask us to delete all of that server’s data.

8. Your choices: opt-out and deletion

Stop the bot from reading your messages

Use /privacy and turn on “Don’t read my messages”. From then on, the bot ignores the content of your ordinary messages everywhere. You can still use slash commands and buttons. You can also use a shortcut by mentioning the bot (@bot где 12) or by replying to a chapter card. Discord always delivers those messages to the bot, so sending one is a direct request to it. Run /privacy again to turn reading back on.

Turn off other features

In /team → “Моё” you can turn off pings, set “Do not disturb” or vacation, hide your rank from your nickname, and edit or remove your payout contacts and “About me”.

Get a copy, correct or delete your data

Message the bot administrator (see Contact). We reply and complete the request within 30 days. When we delete your data, your profile, payout contacts, “About me” and settings are removed. In your team’s chapter and payout history your account is replaced with “deleted user”, so the team’s totals stay correct.

9. Age requirements

You must meet Discord’s minimum age to use Discord and this bot (13, or older where local law requires). The bot is not aimed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes are also announced on the servers that use the bot.

11. Contact

  • Send a direct message on Discord to the bot administrator. Their username is listed in the bot’s Discord profile.
  • Or open an issue in the GitHub repository. Please do not post personal data in a public issue.
