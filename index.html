Discord bot for manga translation teams

DIT | Заливка keeps a manga translation team’s work in one place: chapters, who is on which role, the release schedule, pings, and the team’s balances and payouts.

What it does

Chapters

A card for each chapter, with its roles, statuses, raw files and release time.

Roles and pings

A role scheme decides who gets pinged and when: translation first, then cleaning and typesetting.

Team

Profiles, departments built from server roles, roster images, vacations and “Do not disturb”.

Money

Rates, balances, bonuses and payout records. Records only: the bot never moves money.

Statistics

Top contributors by role, uploads per platform and a release tracker across all titles.

Blueprints

Reviewed per-title scripts that check source sites for new chapters.

How you use it

  • Slash commands and buttons: /team opens the main menu. There are also /balance, /blueprints and admin commands.
  • Text shortcuts in project channels: short messages like создать главу 12, равки 12 with an archive attached, or a reply to a chapter card such as назначить @user Клин.

Your privacy in short

  • The bot reads messages only in project channels, and only to recognise shortcuts. Other messages are ignored and not stored.
  • /privacy stops the bot from reading your messages. You can still use commands, buttons, mentions and replies to cards.
  • Data is never sold or used to train AI. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time.
